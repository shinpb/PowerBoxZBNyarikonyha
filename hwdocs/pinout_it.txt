 - ESP36-C6 Supermini:
   - GND: GND
   - 3V3: 3V3
   - 2: UART_1_TX
   - 3: UART_1_RX (330 Ohm series resistor from the UART_1_RX bus line)
   - 20: DHT1_DATA
   - 19: DHT2_DATA
   - 22: I2C_0_SCL
   - 23: I2C_0_SDA
   - 7: I2C_1_SCL
   - 6: I2C_1_SDA

 - INA3221 [0..3]:
   - VS: 3V3
   - GND: GND
   - SCL: I2C_0_SCL
   - SDA: I2C_0_SDA
   - A0:
     - 0x40: GND
     - 0x41: VS
     - 0x42: SDA
     - 0x43: SCL

 - INA3221 [4]:
   - VS: 3V3
   - GND: GND
   - SCL: I2C_1_SCL
   - SDA: I2C_1_SDA
   - A0:
     - 0x40: GND
     
 - DHT22 [0..1]:
   - "-": GND
   - "+": 3V3
   - out:
     - 0: DHT1_DATA
     - 1: DHT2_DATA

 - UART_1 bus (Modbus-RTU, 9600 8N1; GPIO2/3, not GPIO16/17 = console/USB):
   - UART_1_TX: ESP 2 -> RX of every PZEM (in parallel)
   - UART_1_RX: ESP 3 via 330 Ohm; the bus line has a 10 kOhm pull-up to 3V3
     (UART1 RX, 10 kOhm pull-up to 3V3); each PZEM TX joins it through its own BAT42

 - PZEM-004T v3 [0] (external module, 4 pole connector next to the DHT connectors; Modbus address 0x01):
   - "5V" (logic/optocoupler side): 3V3 (no level shifting needed)
   - GND: GND
   - RX: UART_1_TX
   - TX: BAT42 cathode (stripe at the PZEM TX); BAT42 anode: UART_1_RX bus line
   - further modules [1..2]: same, each with its own BAT42, RX in parallel, other Modbus address
