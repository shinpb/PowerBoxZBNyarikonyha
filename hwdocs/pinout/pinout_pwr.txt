Powerbox:
 - BusBar Blue: 0V
 - BusBar Red: 12V
 - Fuse [0..14]:
   - IN: 12V
   - OUT: FUSED12V[0..14]
 - SW [0..14]:
   - 0: 0V (VIA Fuse 0 - LED 0V, T 1 A)
   - "+": FUSED12V[0..14]
   - out: SW12V[0..14]
 - DCDC UC (ESP32 supply, 5 V output, ~5.2 V in practice):
   - GND: 0V
   - IN: SW12V[0]
   - OUT: SWUC_RAW
 - D1 (1N4001, series, reverse-feed protection of the ESP32 USB/5V pin):
   - ANODE: SWUC_RAW
   - CATHODE (stripe): SWUC
 - DCDC RPI:
   - GND: 0V
   - IN: SW12V[1]
   - OUT: SWRPI
 - METER (INA3221 port) [0..14]:
   - "+":
     - 0: SWUC
     - 1: SWRPI
     - [2..14]: SW12V[2..14]
   - "-":
     - UC_PLUS
     - OUT_PLUS[1..14]
  - OUT_MINUS: 0V
  - ESP32-C6 Supermini:
    - GND: 0V
    - "5V" pin: UC_PLUS   (~5.2 V - ~0.7 V diode drop ~ 4.5 V at the pin)

Periferials:
 - RPI:
   - GND: OUT_MINUS[1]
   - 5V: OUT_PLUS[1]
 - SWITCH:
   - GND: OUT_MINUS[2]
   - 12V: OUT_PLUS[2]
 - POE Uplink:
   - "-": OUT_MINUS[3]
   - "+": OUT_PLUS[3]
 - KAM [0..1]:
   - "-": OUT_MINUS[4..5]
   - "+": OUT_PLUS[4..5]
 - BTR [0..1]:
   - "-": OUT_MINUS[12..13]
   - "+": OUT_PLUS[12..13]
 - CHARGER:
   - "-": OUT_MINUS[14]
   - "+": OUT_PLUS[14]
